# TV provider review

Reviewed 22 October 2026. All **102 proposed additions** were checked individually: **51 retained** and **51 excluded**. All 385 inherited YAML IDs remain addressable, giving **436 catalog identities**.

All 102 proposed additions beyond the inherited 385 YAML IDs. Official TV, broadcaster and video-content services qualify. Generic players, personal media clients, unofficial clients, utilities, radio-only apps and unverified identities are excluded from additions. Identity review does not establish current availability, launch success or Home Assistant/HACS integration.

This corrects an overly broad first implementation of upstream app requests. Exclusion is a scope decision, not an allegation of spam or wrongdoing. Unknown identities were excluded rather than guessed. Generic clients and utilities can still use the card's custom actions; they do not qualify as new provider presets.

Plex Preview supplies Plex programming, talkSPORT supplies an official TV/video channel, and broadcaster apps such as Unser Sandmännchen qualify. Generic IPTV/Plex clients, unofficial YouTube clients, VPNs, cameras and audio-only radio apps do not. Historical official services such as Eurosport Player remain explicitly legacy. The older Beeline TV package is legacy following the provider's announced Popcorn Beeline migration; no replacement package is invented.
